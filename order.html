What Affects Pricing

Final Price Depends On

⚖️
Size & Servings
Larger cakes with more servings cost more. We help you pick the right size.
🎨
Design Complexity
Sculpted and intricate fondant work requires more time and skill.
🍰
Flavour Choice
Some premium flavours (rasmalai, Dutch truffle) may incur a small premium.
✨
Finish Type
Fondant requires more prep time than fresh cream or buttercream finishes.
👑
Custom Toppers
Acrylic, edible gold, or personalised toppers are add-ons.
🚗
Delivery Distance
Delivery fees are distance-based. Pickup is always free.
