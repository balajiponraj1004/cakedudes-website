Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

How early should I order a custom cake?+
For birthday cakes, order 5–7 days in advance. For wedding or tiered cakes, 3–4 weeks is recommended. Urgent 2–3 day requests can often be accommodated — WhatsApp us to check availability.
Are eggless cakes available in all flavours?+
Yes! Every flavour — Black Forest, Chocolate Truffle, Red Velvet, Rasmalai and all others — is available fully eggless with the same premium taste and quality.
What areas do you deliver to?+
We offer pickup from our Mississauga studio (3415 Dixie Rd, Unit 3B) and delivery across the GTA — Toronto, Brampton, Scarborough, Markham, Ajax, Oshawa, Hamilton and surrounding areas.
How much does a custom cake cost?+
Cakes start from CA $60 for a Signature 1-tier. Premium 2–3 tier cakes start from CA $150, and Luxury wedding cakes from CA $350. Final pricing depends on size and design complexity.
Can I send a reference photo for my cake design?+
Absolutely — it's encouraged! Send any inspiration photo via WhatsApp or the order form. We use it as a guide to create something uniquely yours.
Do you make South Asian specialty cakes?+
Yes! We specialize in South Asian flavours (Rasmalai, Cardamom, Mango) and traditional Tamil & Indian celebration cake designs, proudly serving the South Asian community across the GTA.
