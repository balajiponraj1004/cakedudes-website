How We Started

A Passion for Baking That Became a Calling

The Cake Dudes was born from a simple, powerful belief: every celebration deserves a cake as beautiful and meaningful as the moment itself.

Founded by Chef Maruthu, what began as a deep love of baking — experimenting with flavours in a home kitchen, crafting cakes for family and neighbours — grew into something far bigger: a boutique studio with two locations (Mississauga, Canada and Thanjavur, India) where every single cake is treated as a work of art.

"From our very first custom birthday cake to the hundreds of celebrations we've been honoured to be part of — every order is crafted with the same devotion, precision, and love." — Chef Maruthu, Founder

Today, The Cake Dudes serves families across Toronto, Brampton, Mississauga, and the wider GTA — bringing handcrafted custom cakes to celebrations of every size and style, from our studio in Mississauga, Ontario and Thanjavur, Tamil Nadu.

We Speak Your Celebration Language

We understand South Asian celebrations deeply. From vibrant Tamil birthday parties to elegant Indian weddings — we know the colours, the flavours, the scale, and the love that goes into every event.

Whether it's a rasmalai-flavoured anniversary cake, an eggless chocolate truffle for a first birthday pooja, or a three-tier fondant wedding cake to feed 200 guests — we've done it all, and we've done it with joy.

"Our customers aren't just clients. They're families who trust us with their most important moments — and that trust is something we never take lightly."

We're proud to have become the go-to cake studio for South Asian and Tamil families across the Greater Toronto Area and beyond.
