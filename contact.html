Before You Contact Us

Quick Answers

These are the questions we hear most often. Check here first — you might find your answer instantly.

What's the fastest way to reach you?
+
WhatsApp is always fastest. We typically respond within 30 minutes during business hours. For orders, WhatsApp allows you to share reference photos directly too.
Can I visit the studio in person?
+
Yes — but by appointment only. Please WhatsApp or call ahead to schedule your visit. Studio is located at 3415 Dixie Rd, Unit 3B, Mississauga, Ontario.
How do I pay for my order?
+
We accept e-transfer and cash. A 50% deposit is required to confirm your booking date. The balance is due at pickup or delivery.
Do you take last-minute orders?
+
Sometimes! WhatsApp us with your date and requirements — we'll check availability immediately and let you know within the hour.
